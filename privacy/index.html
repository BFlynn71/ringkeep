Privacy policy

Your data stays on your iPhone.

The short version: Ringkeep has no account, no servers and no analytics. The developer collects nothing about you and can’t see your readings.

Effective November 16, 2026

Who we are

Ringkeep is an iPhone app made by Bryan Flynn, an independent developer in Washington State, USA (“I”, “me”). Questions about this policy go to .

What Ringkeep collects

Nothing is collected by the developer. Ringkeep doesn’t send your information to me or to anyone else. It has no account system, no servers, no advertising, no analytics and no third-party code that collects data. Ringkeep makes no internet connections of its own.

What Ringkeep stores on your iPhone

To do its job, Ringkeep keeps the following on your iPhone, in storage that only the app can read:

DataWhere it comes from
Heart rate, blood oxygen, skin and estimated body temperature, sleep stages, steps and activityYour ring, over Bluetooth
Ring estimates of blood pressure, stress and blood sugarYour ring. These are rough estimates, are kept only in Ringkeep, and are never written to Apple Health.
Ring battery level and settingsYour ring
Workouts, including the route and distance of workouts you startYour ring and your iPhone’s location, only while a workout you started is running
Health notes and your personal normalsCalculated on your iPhone from the readings above
Custom ring profilesFiles you choose to import

A summary of recent readings is shared with Ringkeep’s Home Screen widget and, if you use it, the Ringkeep app on your paired Apple Watch. That happens directly between your own devices.

Apple Health

Apple Health access is optional and only happens with your permission.

  • Writing: if you allow it, Ringkeep saves heart rate, blood oxygen, sleep, steps and workouts (with distance and route) to Apple Health.
  • Reading: if you allow it, Ringkeep reads heart rate and steps recorded by your Apple Watch so it can compare your ring with your Watch.
  • Health data is never used for advertising or marketing, never sold, and never shared with anyone.
  • You can change or revoke access at any time in the Health app: tap your profile picture, then Apps, then Ringkeep. You can also turn off saving to Health in Ringkeep’s Ring tab.

Other permissions

  • Bluetooth: to connect to your ring.
  • Location: only to record the route and distance of workouts you start. Ringkeep doesn’t track your location at any other time.
  • Camera and Photos: only for the remote-selfie feature, where tapping your ring takes a photo. Photos are saved to your library. Ringkeep can’t read your existing photos.
  • Notifications: for alerts such as health notes, low battery and your morning summary. These are created on your iPhone.

You can change any of these in the iPhone Settings app.

Backups you export

Ringkeep never backs up your readings automatically. If you tap Export backup, Ringkeep creates a file with your readings and you choose where to save or send it. That file isn’t encrypted, so keep it somewhere you trust. Where it goes after that is up to you and the service you pick.

Purchases

The free trial and Ringkeep Plus are handled by Apple through the App Store. Apple processes the payment. I don’t receive your name, email or payment details. Ringkeep only checks with Apple whether your Apple Account has the trial or Plus.

Keeping and deleting your data

Your data stays on your iPhone until you delete it.

  • In Ringkeep, the Ring tab’s Delete all readings button removes your heart rate and ring history. Workouts can be deleted from the Workouts tab.
  • Deleting the Ringkeep app removes everything it stored on your iPhone and Apple Watch.
  • Anything already saved to Apple Health stays there until you delete it in the Health app.
  • Exported backup files stay wherever you saved them until you delete them.

Because I never receive your data, there’s nothing for me to delete on a server, and no data to sell, share or hand over.

Children

Ringkeep isn’t directed at children under 13, and it doesn’t knowingly collect information from anyone.

Not medical advice

Ringkeep isn’t a medical device. Ring readings aren’t medical-grade, and health notes aren’t a diagnosis. Check with a doctor before making medical decisions.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, I’ll update this page and the effective date above. If a change ever affected how your data is handled, I’d also explain it in the app before it took effect.
