Connecting your ring

Will Ringkeep work with my ring?

Ringkeep is built for inexpensive Bluetooth rings that identify themselves as SMART_RING. Pairing is free, so the quickest test is to download Ringkeep and tap Connect. If it connects, your ring is compatible.

Ringkeep isn’t made, endorsed or supported by any ring manufacturer.

Ringkeep can’t find my ring
  1. Close any other app that connects to your ring, including the app that came with it. The ring talks to one app at a time.
  2. Make sure Bluetooth is on and Ringkeep is allowed to use it: Settings › Privacy & Security › Bluetooth.
  3. Charge the ring for a few minutes and keep it close to your iPhone.
  4. In Ringkeep, open the Ring tab and tap Connect again.
  5. Still stuck? Restart your iPhone and try once more.
My sleep or history didn’t show up

The ring stores readings and hands them over when Ringkeep connects. Open Ringkeep in the morning with the ring nearby and give it a minute to sync. Sleep appears after you’ve worn the ring overnight.

Can I use a different ring?

If you’re technically minded, you can add a ring with a profile file that describes its Bluetooth signals. Open the Ring tab › Custom rings for a step-by-step guide. Custom rings support battery, heart rate, temperature and oxygen, but not sleep. Other rings aren’t guaranteed to work.

Trial and purchases

What’s free and what’s in Ringkeep Plus?

Always free: pairing, live heart rate, today’s readings and demo mode.

Ringkeep Plus ($4.99, one time): history and trends, sleep history, health notes, workouts, Apple Health sync, the Apple Watch app, Ring vs Watch and custom ring profiles.

How does the free trial work?

Tap Start 7-day free trial on any Plus screen. Everything is unlocked for 7 days and there’s nothing to pay. When the trial ends, the Plus features lock until you unlock Ringkeep Plus. Nothing you recorded is deleted. Each Apple Account gets one trial.

I bought Ringkeep Plus on another iPhone

Open any Plus screen, or the Ringkeep Plus card on the Ring tab, and tap Restore Purchases. Make sure you’re signed in with the same Apple Account you used to buy it.

How do I get a refund?

Purchases are handled by Apple. You can request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Your data

Where is my data stored?

Only on your iPhone, and on your Apple Watch if you use the Watch app. Ringkeep has no account and no servers. If you turn on Apple Health sync, the readings you allow are also saved to Apple Health. See the privacy policy for details.

How do I back up or move to a new iPhone?
  1. On the Ring tab, tap Export backup and save the file in Files (or anywhere you like).
  2. On your new iPhone, install Ringkeep, open the Ring tab and tap Restore from file.

Restoring merges, so nothing already on the phone is lost. Export now and then so your backup stays current.

How do I connect or disconnect Apple Health?

Ringkeep asks the first time it runs. To change it later, open the Health app, tap your profile picture, then Apps › Ringkeep. You can also turn off saving to Health in Ringkeep’s Ring tab.

How do I delete my data?

On the Ring tab, tap Delete all readings. Workouts can be deleted from the Workouts tab. Deleting the app removes everything Ringkeep stored. Readings already saved to Apple Health stay there until you remove them in the Health app.

Readings and accuracy

How accurate are the readings?

Ringkeep shows what your ring measures. Budget ring sensors aren’t medical-grade, and readings can be off if the ring is loose or moving. A snug fit on a finger you don’t use much helps.

What are health notes?

After about 14 days, Ringkeep learns your normal resting heart rate, overnight oxygen, temperature and sleep. If one drifts outside your normal for a few days, you’ll see a note. Notes aren’t a diagnosis. If something keeps happening or you feel unwell, check with a doctor.
