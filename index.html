Compatibility

Will it work with my ring?

Ringkeep is built for one family of inexpensive Bluetooth rings that identify themselves as SMART_RING. The easiest way to know for sure costs nothing:

  1. Download Ringkeep. Pairing is always free.
  2. Close any other app that connects to your ring. The ring talks to one app at a time.
  3. Tap Connect. If Ringkeep finds and connects to your ring, it’s compatible.

No ring yet? You can explore the whole app with sample data first.

Not affiliated with any ring maker. Ringkeep is an independent app. It isn’t made, endorsed or supported by the manufacturer of any ring. Technically minded users can add other rings with a profile file, but other rings aren’t guaranteed to work.

Questions

Good to know

Is Ringkeep a medical device?

No. Ringkeep shows readings from your ring’s sensors, which aren’t medical-grade and can be off if the ring is loose. Health notes point out changes from your own normal. They aren’t a diagnosis. Check with a doctor before making any medical decision.

What happens if I get a new iPhone?

Export a backup from the Ring tab and save it in Files. On your new iPhone, choose Restore from file. Restoring merges, so nothing already on the phone is lost.

Do I need an Apple Watch?

No. The Watch app is a bonus if you have one. Everything works on iPhone alone.

Which iPhones are supported?

Any iPhone running iOS 17 or later.
