Compatibility

Tested rings

Ringkeep is built around one ring family’s Bluetooth protocol. Other rings can be added with a profile file. This page lists what’s actually confirmed working, not guessed at.

Built in

These work out of the box, with no setup.

SMART_RING family rings

The ring Ringkeep was built for. These inexpensive rings identify themselves as SMART_RING over Bluetooth.

  • Heart rate, live and automatic
  • Blood oxygen and skin temperature
  • Sleep, steps and activity history
  • Battery level

Not sure if yours is one? Pairing is free: download Ringkeep and tap Connect. If it connects, it works.

Added by the community

These rings were added by someone who decoded the ring’s Bluetooth signals and shared a profile file. Support is partial: battery, heart rate, temperature and oxygen. Sleep and step history aren’t supported for custom rings yet. Custom rings need Ringkeep Plus or the free trial.

Got a ring that isn’t listed?
